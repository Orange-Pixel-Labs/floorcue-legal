FAQ

Does FloorCue provide music?

FloorCue provides curated song metadata and external search helpers only. It does not stream, download, distribute, or license music.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use FloorCue without an account. Sign in is optional and enables sync.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks Competition Prep (readiness, checklist, mock rounds), advanced progress insights, and curated song suggestions by dance. Core practice planning, notes, and the dance library stay free.

How do I send feedback?

Use the in-app feedback flow from Home or Settings → Legal & Support.
